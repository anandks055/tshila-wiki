## Solar Dynasty

The Solar Dynasty is a prominent royal lineage in ancient Indian mythology, featuring several esteemed figures. According to the Skanda Purana, King Veṇu is associated with this lineage [1]. Additionally, Daśaratha, an important figure in Hindu epics, is also noted as belonging to this illustrious dynasty [2].

The Devi Bhagavata Purana further emphasizes the noble heritage and royal connections within the Solar Dynasty. King Dhruvasandhi is highlighted as a member of this lineage, showcasing his noble birth and heritage [1]. Similarly, Manoramā's husband Dhruvasandhi is also noted to belong to this esteemed dynasty, indicating their royal lineage [2]. Furthermore, it is mentioned that Hariścandra’s son is part of the Solar Dynasty, emphasizing his noble birth as well [3].
