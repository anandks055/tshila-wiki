## Overview

Kurukṣetra is an ancient site established by Kuru, son of Samvaraṇa, located between the rivers Sarasvatī and Dṛśadvatī. It has been a significant region for both religious and historical reasons in Indian culture [1][2]. The city is renowned as the battlefield where the great war described in the Mahābhārata took place, involving the Pāṇdavas and Kauravas, triggered by a gambling incident and marked by intense moral dilemmas faced by Arjuna who sought guidance from Krishna. This region also witnessed significant events such as the teaching of the Bhagavadgītā to Arjuna [3].

## Religious Significance

Kurukṣetra is considered a sacred 'dharmakṣetra' or field of righteousness, being associated with numerous yāgas (sacrifices) performed by gods and serving as the heart of Vedic culture in ancient times. The region was known to remove sins for those who lived there [4][5]. It has been recognized as a holy place where taking a bath during solar eclipses is recommended, and śrāddha ceremonies are often performed [6].

## Historical Context

The city was founded by Kuru, son of Samvaraṇa, and later became the capital city of the Kurus. The region is known for its various historical events such as the battles involving Sauvira, Dāsarṇa, Matsyas, Pahlavas, and Asmakas [7][8]. It also includes notable locations like Hastināpura (65 miles northwest of Delhi), Indraprastha (part of old Delhi), Sthāṇeśvara (present-day Thanesar in Haryana), and Pṛthudaka (present-day Pehova) [2].

## Sacred Sites

Kurukṣetra is home to several revered sites such as the Kurukṣetra Lake, which was originally five pools of blood converted into a single holy water lake by Paraśurāma’s penance. It is also noted for its spiritual significance through places like Varāhatīrtha (where Viṣṇu stood in his boar incarnation), Vyāsatirtha (where Vyāsa wanted to give up his body but was dissuaded), and Saptasarasvata (where Śiva appeared before the sage Maṅkaṇaka) [2].

## Modern Kurukshetra

Today, Kurukṣetra is a town in Haryana, India, part of the Ambala division within the Kurukshetra district. It has a rich educational infrastructure with institutions like Wisdom World School and Kurukshetra University. The region also boasts several religious sites including Guru Ravidass Temple and Sthaneshwar Mahadev Temple [9].
