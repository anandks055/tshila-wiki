## Draupadi

Draupadi was a prominent figure in ancient Indian history and literature. She was the daughter of King Drupada of Kampilya and chose five Pandava brothers as her husbands during a Svayamvara ceremony, where brides select their own grooms [1]. This event is central to her role in the epic narrative.

In addition to her marital relationship with the Pandavas, Draupadi's life was marked by significant conflicts. She became a target of Kicaka, whose actions led to his death at the hands of Bhīmasena [2][3].

Draupadi is primarily known as the main female character in the Mahābhārata, one of India’s great epics [4]. However, her name also refers to a constellation located between Cassiopeia and Pisces, which includes the Andromeda galaxy M31 [5].

Her family ties are further illustrated through her brother Dhṛṣṭadyumna, who is referred to as “son of Drupada” in the epic narratives [6].
