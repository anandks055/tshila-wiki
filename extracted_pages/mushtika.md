## Overview of Mushtika

Mushtika is a prominent figure in Hindu mythology, primarily known as an Asura and wrestler associated with the evil king Kamsa. He was one of the most powerful wrestlers in Kamsa's court and was tasked by Kamsa to challenge Krishna and Balarāma in a wrestling match during the Dhanuryāga sacrifice at Mathura.

### Role and Encounter

Mushtika was invited by Kamsa along with Cāṇura, another formidable wrestler, to fight against Krishna and Balarāma. In this encounter, Mushtika took his seat in the enclosure where the wrestling match would take place and challenged Rama (Balarāma). The match was conducted according to established rules, but it ended tragically for Mushtika as he was ultimately defeated and killed by Balarāma during the bout.

### Mythological Context

In various Puranas such as the Harivamsha Purana and Vishnu Purana, Mushtika is portrayed as an enemy of the gods. The Harivamsha Purana describes him as a formidable wrestler destined to be defeated by Madhusudana (a name for Krishna), while in the Vishnu Purana, he is listed among the demons slain by Krishna.

The Brahma Purana and Bhagavata Purana also recount Mushtika's role in Kamsa’s plans to oppress the Yadus. In these texts, Mushtika is described as a wrestler who was challenged by Balarāma and eventually defeated. The Bhagavata Purana specifically notes that he competed against Balarāma in the wrestling arena and was killed during this encounter.
