## Trinavarta

Trinavarta is a demon in Hindu mythology known for his ability to assume the form of a whirlwind. His primary notoriety stems from an incident where he attempted to carry off the infant Krishna, but was ultimately overpowered and killed by the child himself. This event highlights both the divine strength of Krishna even at a young age and the role of demons in Hindu tales as antagonists that serve to demonstrate the heroism and power of deities.
