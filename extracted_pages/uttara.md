## Uttara

### Definitions and Meanings

The term "Uttara" has multiple definitions across various contexts:

1. **Directional Context**: Refers to the north direction, protected by Kubera [1], indicating one of three spiritual paths in Pārānanda sūtra that values commands from a guru alongside the Veda [2]. Additionally, in historical geography, it refers to the northern part of Gujarat and is also used as the name of King Virata's daughter who was married to Abhimanyu [1][3].

2. **Philosophical Context**: In Mimāmsā philosophy, "uttara" denotes an answer or response given in an adhikaraṇa, which acts as part of structured analysis [3]. It also signifies the north direction and is associated with spiritual paths valuing guru commands alongside Vedic texts.

3. **Grammar**: Refers to something following or subsequent, such as the latter part of a compound word (uttarapada) [5].

4. **Astrological Context**: According to the Garuda Purana’s Agastya Samhita, Uttara is a star of the Vishvadevatas and is favorable for installing a king [1][1].

### Historical and Cultural Significance

- In Hindu mythology, Uttara is the father of Irāvati and the father-in-law of Parīkṣit [SOURCE 1].
- Additionally, in the context of ancient India, Uttara refers to the daughter of King Virata from the Matsya kingdom who was married to Abhimanyu, son of Arjuna [4][3].

### Linguistic Usage

In Sanskrit and Kannada:
- **Sanskrit**: The term "uttara" can be translated as a subsequent or following part of something. It denotes an answer in philosophical discourse and refers to the north direction [5].
- **Kannada (uttara)**: Terms meaning situated far above ground, of good quality, later/following, and excessive are associated with it [SOURCE 8].

### Geographical Locations

1. **Andaman and Nicobar Islands**: Uttara is a village in Rangat tehsil within North And Middle Andaman district. It features schools like the Govt. Sec. School Nimbutala North and Lord Shiva Temple Bakultala, among others [SOURCE 9].
2. **Odisha**: In Bhubaneswar city, Uttara encompasses localities such as Balisahi, Balisahi Road, Banamalipur Road, and Dhauli Road. These areas are known for their proximity to temples like Maa Tarini and Jagannath Temple [SOURCE 9].

### Astrological and Astronomical References

In astrology, Uttara is mentioned in the Garuda Purana's Agastya Samhita as a star of the Vishvadevatas favorable for installing kings [1][1].
