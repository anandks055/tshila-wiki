## Mithila

### Historical and Mythological Context

Mithila is a city and region renowned for its beauty and significance in ancient Indian lore, primarily within the context of the epic Ramayana. It is governed by King Janaka and revered by ascetics who often rest there during their travels [1]. In addition to its spiritual importance, Mithila is also notable as the location where a divine bow is kept, which many kings attempt to string in order to win Sītā's hand in marriage [2].

The region is also known by another name: Vaideha [3], and is associated with King Janaka. According to some sources, Mithila was founded by a king named Mithi or Mithila, making it the capital of Videha or modern-day Tirhut [6].

### Etymology and Historical References

The name "Mithila" has various etymological roots and historical mentions in ancient texts. In Sanskrit, "mithilā" refers to both a city and a people. The term is associated with King Janaka, who is also known as the lord of Mithila [6].

In Bharati Kosha, there are references to Janakamaharaj and his connection to Mithila through various epithets such as "Videhas" and "Mithilā" [2]. Additionally, in some ancient texts like the Bhagavata Purana, Mithila is mentioned as being associated with King Janaka [3].

The term “mithilā” has been used to denote both a people and their founder. This can be seen in sources such as Macdonell's Sanskrit-English Dictionary (1893) and Monier-Williams' Sanskrit-English Dictionary (1899), where it is described as the name of a king and a city [4,6].

### Modern Context

In contemporary times, Mithila also refers to a village in the tehsil of Beltangadi, located within Dakshin Kannad district in Karnataka, India. This village has a population of 5,196 residing across 900 houses over an area of 1,477 hectares [9]. It is surrounded by various taluks and near several important rivers like the Neriya Hole and Nandini. The local language spoken in this region is Tulu.

The village is known for its religious significance due to the presence of temples such as the Padala Subrahmanya Temple and Mallikarjuna Temple [9]. It also boasts educational facilities including the Government Higher Primary School Andethadka Ilanthila [9].
