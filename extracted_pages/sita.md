## Sita

### Definitions and Meanings

**Sita**, in various linguistic contexts, carries a range of meanings:

1. **Adjective Forms:**
   - *White*: Describes something with the color akin to pure snow. [3]
   - *Bound/Tied*: Refers to being fastened or tied as if by a rope. [3]
   - *Surrounded*: Used to denote encircled or surrounded entities. [3]
   - *Discovered/Found Out*: Indicates something that has been uncovered or known. [3]
   - *Completed/Finished*: Describes processes or states that have reached their conclusion. [3]

2. **Noun Forms:**
   - *White Color*: Represents the hue of pure snow. [4]
   - *Morning Star (Venus)*: Refers to the planet Venus visible before sunrise in the eastern sky, often called the daystar. [4]
   - *Crystalline Sugar*: Denotes a type of sugar with crystallized form. [4]
   - *Arrow*: Represents an arrow or projectile used in archery. [4]
   - *Silver*: Used to denote the precious metal silver. [4]
   - *Lunar Fortnight*: Refers to the period between the new moon and full moon when the moon's phase increases. [4]
   - *Sandalwood (Santalum album)*: The aromatic wood from the Santalaceae family. [4]
   - *Acacia catechu Tree*: A tree species belonging to the Mimosae family. [4]

### Historical and Cultural References

In Hindu mythology, Sita is mentioned in various contexts:
- **Warrior of Subrahmaṇya**: According to the Mahābhārata, a warrior named Sita serves under Subrahmaṇya (also known as Skanda). [6][7]
- **Epithet for Balarāma**: The term "Sita" can also denote an epithet for Balarāma. [8]

### Geographical Locations

**Sita** is recognized as a village or town in different parts of India:
1. **Thoubal District, Manipur**: A village located near Jiribam railway station and Imphal Airport with pincode 795135. [9]
2. **Tengnoupal Tehsil, Chandel District, Manipur**: Another Sita is situated in the same district, surrounded by rivers such as Yu River and Sekmai Turel, featuring educational institutions like St. Anthonys School Machi with pincode 795135. [9]
3. **Weir Tehsil, Bharatpur District, Rajasthan**: A village with a pincode of 321409, known for temples such as Hatauri Hanuman Mandir and rivers like Gambhir Panchai Nadi. [9]
