Vamana is an avatar of Vishnu in Hindu mythology known for his role in reclaiming earth from the demon king Bali, who had gained immense power. Bali was a powerful ruler and grandson of Prahlada, known for his generosity but also for conquering all three worlds, causing distress among other gods. Vamana approached Bali during an elaborate sacrifice, requesting just three steps of land as alms. Using his divine powers, Vamana grew to cosmic proportions and reclaimed the earth with two steps, leaving no space for the third step, which Bali honored by offering himself.
