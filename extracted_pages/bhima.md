## Bhima: An Overview

Bhima is a multifaceted figure in Hindu mythology, literature, and geography. In the context of the Mahābhārata epic, Bhima is one of the Pāṇḍava brothers, known for his immense strength and loyalty to his brother Yudhiṣṭhira and Śrī Kṛṣṇa [1][2]. His father was Vāyu, the deity of air, making Bhima one of Vāyu's sons [3].

### Mythological and Literary References

In various texts such as the Shiva Purana and Garuda Purana, Bhima is revered in different contexts. In the Shiva Purana, he appears as an eightfold manifestation of Śiva, symbolizing ether or space [4][5]. He also features as a powerful demon son of Kumbhakarṇa who was eventually defeated by Śiva [6].

In the Garuda Purana, Bhima is mentioned for his role in killing Duryodhana during the Mahābhārata war and is known to have fasted on Ekadashi to free himself from obligations to the Pitris [7][8]. Additionally, he is referred to as Bhimasena in some texts.

### Geographical References

Bhima also refers to a river that serves as a significant tributary of the Kṛṣṇa River. This river forms part of the geographical boundaries of regions like Kuntala and flows through areas such as Hyderabad and Madras [9]. It is associated with a town containing a celebrated shrine of Vithoba on its right bank.

### Historical and Lineage References

In various genealogical records, Bhima has been linked to different lineages. For example, he is noted as the son of Vijaya and father of Kāñcana [10]. In other texts like the Harivamsha Purana, Bhima is mentioned in multiple roles, including one of Amavasu’s sons and the father of Kancanaprabha [11][12].

### Modern Locations

There are several places named Bhima across India. Notably, there is a village named Bhima in Baragudha tehsil, Sirsa district, Haryana, with a pincode of 125078, located approximately 17 kilometers north of Sirsa [13]. Another Bhima exists in Palasi tehsil, Araria district, Bihar, with a pincode of 854311 and situated about 13 kilometers east of Araria.
