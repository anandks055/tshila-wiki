## Vibhishana: A Devoted Follower of Rama

Vibhishana, the youngest brother of Ravana, Surpanakha, and Kumbhakarna, is a significant figure in Hindu mythology known for his devotion to Rama. He questioned how to destroy heinous sins through the repetition of Rama's name in the Rāmarahasya Upaniṣad [1]. Vibhishana is also noted for singing hymns in praise of Rama in the Adhyatma Ramayana [2].

### Role in Rama's Victory
Vibhishana deserted his brother Ravana to join Rama, providing invaluable assistance that ensured Rama’s victory and the restoration of dharma [3]. After Ravana's defeat, he offered the flying chariot Puṣpaka to Rama to help him return to Ayodhya [4].

### Pilgrimage Sites
Vibhishana is associated with a temple site in Dhanushkoti and is said to have visited many pilgrimage sites according to sthalapurāṇas [5][6]. He is also recognized for carrying the icon of Ranganatha from Ayodhya to Lanka but leaving it at Srirangam [7][8].

### Assistance in Sita's Rescue
Vibhishana helped Hanuman locate Sita after recognizing her presence in Lanka [9] and assisted Rama’s army by guiding them across the ocean and serving as a counselor. He also fought valiantly against formidable opponents like Prahasta and Dhumraksha, revealing the invisibility of Ravana's forces with celestial weapons [5][6]. Throughout the conflict, he played a crucial role in awakening Rama and Lakshmana, bringing healing water from Kuvera, guiding Lakshmana in fighting against Indrajit, and encouraging Rama to use Indra’s chariot to defeat Ravana [7].

### Post-Ravana
After Ravana's death, Vibhishana was installed as the ruler of Lanka by Rama. He continued to support Rama even after the battle ended, consoling the demons and performing Ravana’s cremation [2][8]. Additionally, he is noted for advising against harming Hanuman in the Agni Purana [1].

### Devotion and Immortality
Vibhishana is described as a great king who assisted Ravana in ruling Lanka but later became a devoted follower of Rama. He was rewarded with heaven and granted immortality by Brahma [1][10]. Vibhishana’s devotion to Vishnu is highlighted in various texts, where he is mentioned alongside Indra and Rama as having worshipped Vishnu [1].
