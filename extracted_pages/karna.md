## Karna

### Definitions

The term "Karna" can be understood both linguistically and geographically:

1. **Linguistic Definition**: In Kannada, the term "ಕರ್ನ" (phonetic notation: karna) refers to the organ of hearing and balance in humans and vertebrates, particularly emphasizing the external part of this organ, which is commonly known as the ear. Additionally, it can denote corresponding organs in other animals. This definition is derived from Kannada language dictionaries. [1]

### Geographical Context

**Karna Village, Haryana**: Karna is a village located within Palwal Tehsil, which itself is part of Palwal District in the state of Haryana, India. The village is noted for its proximity to several religious and educational landmarks such as Shiv Mandir OM SAI COLONY and Hanuman Mandir Bihari Colony, along with educational institutions like Golaya Progressive Public School and Bainsla Niketan. It also has a postal code of 121102. [2]

**Karna in Other Locations**: Additionally, there are other villages named Karna located across different states in India, including the Jamui District of Bihar, where it is surrounded by rivers like the Barakar River, and Khagaria District in Bihar, which is close to the Burhi Gandak Sun Kosi river. Each of these locations has its own unique cultural and geographical features, such as nearby cities and villages. [2]
