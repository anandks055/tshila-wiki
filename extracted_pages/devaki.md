## Devaki

### Overview

Devaki refers to two distinct villages located in different parts of India. The first village is situated in Jalore Tehsil, within the Jalore District of Rajasthan. This village is approximately 14 kilometers north of the district headquarters at Jalore and is known for its religious sites such as the Jagnath Mahadev Temple and Hanumanji Mandir. It is surrounded by rivers including Kota Ka Bala Jawai. The second Devaki is located in Ghaghra Block, within the Gumla District of Jharkhand. This village features temples like Dewaki Dham and Jay Mahakal Baba Bhole and is situated near rivers such as Bandi Koel Nadi and Sankh River.

### Historical Context

In historical records, Devaki also refers to a ruler who defeated Devasena, the king of Suhmas, as documented in the Harsacarita. This event provides an insight into early Indian history and politics, highlighting conflicts between regional rulers.

### Geographical Details

The village located in Rajasthan has a pin code of 307029 and is near other villages such as Badanwari and Debawas. The Devaki in Jharkhand also has its own distinct geographical features with a pin code of 835231.
