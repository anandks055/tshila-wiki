## Aghāsura

Aghāsura is a demon in Hindu mythology who appears in various contexts within ancient texts. According to the *Bhagavata Purana*, he is defeated by Kṛṣṇa, an event that was witnessed by Brahmā [1]. Additionally, in the same text, Aghāsura is described as a boa-constrictor and mentioned among the threats from which Kṛṣṇa protected the inhabitants of Vraja [2].

In the *Skanda Purana*, Aghāsura is known for being slain by Śaṃbhu (Shiva) and used as a title for Shiva when addressing Bhānu, the Sun God [2]. Furthermore, he is referenced in the depiction of Śaṅkara holding the Khaṭvāṅga, symbolizing his protective role [1].

In Dowson's work on Hindu mythology, Aghāsura is described as an Asura who served as a general for Kansa. He assumed the form of a vast serpent, and Krishna’s companions, the cowherds, mistakenly entered its mouth, thinking it was a mountain cavern; however, Krishna rescued them [3].
