The Disrobing of Draupadi is an episode from the Indian epic Mahabharata, depicting a scene where Draupadi, one of the central characters and wife to the Pandava brothers, is forcibly disrobed by the Kaurava prince Duryodhana's younger brother, Dushasana. This act symbolizes humiliation and is pivotal in driving the narrative towards the Kurukshetra war.
