## Shatrughna: Overview and Context

Shatrughna is a significant figure in Hindu mythology, often recognized as the youngest brother of Lord Rama. Born to Queen Sumitrā, he plays pivotal roles across various texts including the ānanda Rāmāyana, Garuda Purana, Harivamsha Purana, Yoga Vasistha, Linga Purana, and Vishnu Purana.

### Family and Lineage

Shatrughna is one of the four sons of King Dasharatha, making him an incarnation of Vishnu according to the Garuda Purana [1]. He is also described as a son of Śvaphalka and Gāndinī in the Vishnu Purana [2].

### Mythological Roles

In the ānanda Rāmāyana, he is noted for killing the demon Lavaṇāsura [3] and is mentioned as the father of Yupaketu [1][2]. The Garuda Purana also highlights his victory over a rakshasa named Lavana [4].

The Harivamsha Purana mentions Shatrughna in various contexts, including being part of Krausthu’s family lineage and participating in an acting performance [5][6].

In the Yoga Vasistha, he is portrayed as closely mirroring Rama's habits and demeanor. He is depicted as a joyful individual who glows like the full moon after receiving teachings from Vasishtha, eventually expressing contentment in the supreme soul post-discourse [7][8][9][10][11].

### Geographical Contributions

The Vishnu Purana describes Shatrughna founding the city of Mathura after defeating a rakshasa named Lavana [12]. This event is also noted in the abridged version of the Vishnu Purana [13][14].
