Vishwamitra is a revered sage in Hindu mythology known for his transformation from a king to one of the most respected rishis (sages). He is famous for his intense penances and spiritual achievements, which earned him the title of "Brahmarishi." Vishwamitra is also recognized as one of the principal composers of the Rigveda and plays a significant role in various Hindu texts such as the Ramayana.
