## Definition

**Vali**, derived from Sanskrit and Kannada languages, has multiple meanings across different contexts. In the context of anatomy or physical appearance, **vali** refers to folds of skin or wrinkles (Sources 1-7). Specifically in Kannada, it denotes any of three specific folds above a woman's navel (Source 2).

In a broader sense, **vali** can also mean:
- A wave-like formation on the surface of water bodies caused by internal or external disturbances (Source 2).
- A thin line connecting two points on the same plane (Source 2).
- The act of plucking a string instrument with a jerking movement to produce regulated oscillations for aesthetic effect in music (Source 2).

Additionally, **vali** can also denote:
- A sage or ascetic (Source 3).
- An opportunity for action that comes in rotation among a number of individuals or entities; essentially a turn (Source 4).

In terms of its etymological and ritualistic significance, **vali** refers to offerings, tributes, and oblations, often made with fragrant unguents on the person, indicating natural lines or folds of skin (Sources 7-8). This includes offerings to spirits of air and making lines or streaks on the body with perfumed oils.

## Geographical Locations

Vali also refers to administrative units located in different regions:
- In Rajasthan, Vali is an administrative unit within the tehsil of Bhinmal in Jalor district and another instance in Jalore district, both sharing a pincode of 343030 (Source 9).
- There is another Vali situated in the tehsil of Jhagadia in Bharuch district, Gujarat. This region is notable for its proximity to temples such as Bhathiji Mandir and Chamunda Maa Temple, along with educational institutions like schools and colleges within a short distance (Source 9).
