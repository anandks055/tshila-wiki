## Narasimha

Narasimha, an incarnation of Vishnu (referred to as Hari), was specifically manifested to kill Hiraṇyakaśipu using neither wet nor dry nails. This avatār was invoked after the gods approached him following Brahmā granting Hiraṇyakaśipu's request for protection. Narasimha promised to slay Hiraṇyakaśipu and proceeded to his abode where only Prahlāda recognized Him as the supreme Lord. During the confrontation, all the Asuras attacked Narasimha from every direction; however, Narasimha ultimately killed Hiraṇyakaśipu by tearing him apart with His teeth [1].
