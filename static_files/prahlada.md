## Prahlada

### Historical Figure in Hinduism

Prahlada, a celebrated devotee of Lord Narasimha, is renowned for his unwavering devotion and is commemorated in stotras such as the Prahlada Sthuthi. His dedication to Lord Narasimha exemplifies the deep spiritual commitment that resonates through many Hindu devotional texts. The story of Prahlada serves as an inspiration for countless devotees who seek spiritual guidance and strength. [1]

### Geographical Location

Prahlada is also the name of a small village/hamlet located within the Gilaula block of Shravasti district in Uttar Pradesh, India. Administratively, it falls under the Prahlada Panchayat and belongs to the Devipatan division. The village is approximately 29 kilometers west of the Shravasti district headquarters and about 138 kilometers from Lucknow, the state capital. It is situated on the border between Shravasti and Bahraich districts.

The surrounding blocks include Hariharpur Rani, Visheshwarganj, Chitaura, and Prayagpur. Nearby cities such as Bahraich, Balrampur, Nanpara, and Tulsipur are significant landmarks in the region. The village boasts several religious sites, including the Shree Thakur Baba Mandir and Samay Mata Mandir, reflecting its rich cultural heritage. Additionally, educational institutions like UPS Kalyanpur and Shree Kashi Vish Nath I C Khaira Kalawan contribute to the community's development.

Hindi is the primary language spoken in Prahlada village, underscoring its linguistic identity within the region. [2]
