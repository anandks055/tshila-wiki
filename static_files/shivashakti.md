## Etymology

Śiva Śakti (Kannada: ಶಿವಶಕ್ತಿ) is a term that refers to Pārvati as the female primordial energy of the universe [1]. It also denotes divine power and is associated with the plant Gloriosa superba, known colloquially as "tiger's claw" [1].

## Shruti

In Indic knowledge traditions, Śiva Śakti represents the cosmic feminine energy, Pārvati. She embodies the primordial creative force that sustains and governs the universe [1]. 

## Smriti

Śiva Śakti is also recognized as a manifestation of divine power in various texts, underscoring her role as an essential aspect of the cosmos [1].

## Kavya

The term Śiva Śakti can also refer to the plant Gloriosa superba (Liliaceae family), which is known locally as "tiger's claw" [1].
