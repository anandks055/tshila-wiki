## Shivashakti

**Shivashakti** (Kannada: ಶಿವಶಕ್ತಿ; phonetic notation: Śiva śakti) is a term with multiple connotations within Indic knowledge traditions [1]. It refers to:

1. **Pārvati**: Pārvati, as the female primordial energy of the universe [1].
2. **Divine Power**: The divine power itself, embodying the cosmic feminine force [1].
3. **Gloriosa superba**: Additionally, it denotes the plant *Gloriosa superba* of the Liliaceae family, commonly known as tiger's claw [1].
