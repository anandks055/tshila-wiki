## Ravana

### Mythological Figure

In Hindu mythology, **Ravana** is depicted as the son of Dharma (Vasu) [1]. This representation situates him within a lineage tied to cosmic order and righteousness in ancient Indian literature. However, beyond this familial association, no additional mythological details are provided by the sources.

### Geographical Locations

The name "Ravana" also refers to several geographical locations across India:

#### Ravana (Dhari tehsil)

A village named **Ravana** is located within the Dhari tehsil of Amreli district in Gujarat, India. It has a postal code of 365630 and is known for its religious sites such as the Udasin Asharm Khisa Khicha and Narmadeshwar Mahadev Temple [2]. The village is situated near rivers like Hiran Ghagaria. Schools in this area include Saint John English Medium School and Shree G.N. Damani Municipal High School.

#### Ravana (Shahabad tehsil)

Another **Ravana** is found in Shahabad tehsil, Rampur district, Uttar Pradesh, India. This village has a pincode of 244922 [2]. It features religious landmarks such as the Temple Pasupura and Shiv Temple Pasupura. The village is surrounded by rivers like the East Bahgul Nadi and Gaula River. Educational facilities in this area include Sarkari School Ravana and Netaji Subash Vidya Mandir Primary Pathshala.

#### Ravana (Danagadi tehsil)

Lastly, there exists a hamlet named **Ravana** located within Danagadi tehsil of Jajapur district, Odisha, India. This place has the pincode 755026 [2]. It is known for its religious sites such as the Hanuman Temple and Sairam Mandir and is surrounded by the river Gonda Salandi.

### Conclusion

The name "Ravana" thus encompasses a mythological figure from Hindu mythology, along with several locations in India that are notable for their cultural and geographical features.
