## Overview

Barsana is a village located in Charkhi Dadri Tehsil, Bhiwani District, Haryana, India. It holds significant religious importance due to its connection with Hindu pilgrimage sites within the Braj Mandala region, which includes Mathura and Vrndavana [1]. Additionally, Barsana serves as an important reference point for locating Govardhana, being approximately 27 kilometers south of Bhiwani and about 14 miles from Govardhana [2].

## Religious Significance

Barsana is associated with notable religious festivals such as Kartik Purnima and Radha Ashtami (Bhadrapada Shukla 8) [3]. It also holds historical significance due to its connection with Jagadguru Shrivallabhaacharya, who conducted assemblies and compassionate activities at Gahvarvan within Barsana, imparting spiritual teachings [4].

## Nearby Locations

Barsana is situated near other villages such as Atela Khurd and Birhi Kalan. It is also close to cities like Bhiwani and Mahendragarh. Additionally, there are other locations named Barsana in Mathura, Bharatpur, Hardoi, and Kaithal, each with its own unique features and administrative details.

## Religious Sites

The village has notable temples such as the Dadi Masani Temple, adding to its religious importance within the region [1].

## Transportation

Barsana is connected by motor buses to nearby sites like Nandgaon, facilitating pilgrimages and travel within the Braj Mandala region [1].
