## Kapila: Sage and Incarnation

Kapila is a revered ṛṣi (sage) in Hindu tradition, recognized as an incarnation of Viṣṇu. He is the son of Kardama and Devahuti [28] and is primarily known for founding Sāṅkhya philosophy, which explains the origins of the universe through the dualism of purusha and prakriti [9]. Kapila taught this philosophical system to his mother Devahuti and later to his disciples such as Asuri and Pañcaśikha [12, 14].

Kapila is also known for a significant event in Hindu mythology where he reduced the sixty thousand sons of King Sagara into ashes with just a glance. This act prompted the quest to bring the Gaṅgā (Ganges) to Earth for their redemption [5]. He lived in various locations including Haridvāra and Gaṅgāsāgara, and his hermitage was the site of many significant events in Hindu mythology [7, 8].

Kapila is depicted with specific characteristics such as a crown-like hairstyle (jaṭāmukuṭa), beard, and often portrayed with four arms—two holding a pitcher (symbolizing yoga) and the other two holding a conch shell (śaṅkha) and discus (cakra). He is also listed among the parivāradevatās for Viṣṇu temples [14].

Kapila's teachings were compiled in works like the Sāñkhyasutras and Sāñkhyakārikās, and his mantras are included in texts such as the Jayākhya Samhitā [20, 24]. Additionally, he is mentioned as one of the sages who attended gatherings to hear Śiva's teachings [26].

## Kapila: Geographical Context

Kapila has several geographical references. In historical and geographical contexts, Kapila was a saint who visited Śrāvasti for knowledge acquisition [1][2]. He is also associated with one of the eight holy places mentioned in the Brahmapurana [3], and according to the Skandapurana, Kapila refers to a river marking the eastern boundary of Utkala [4].

In modern times, there are several locations named Kapila. One notable village is located in Binjharpur Tehsil, Jajpur District, Odisha, India, with a pin code of 755004. It is situated 19 kilometers south of the district headquarters, Jajpur, and is near religious sites like Hanuman Temple Taljanga Mandhatapatna and MAA KARANJEI TEMPLE. Another Kapila exists in Bansi Tehsil, Siddharth Nagar District, Uttar Pradesh, located near rivers such as the Rohini. A third location named Kapila is found in Bhandaripokhari Tehsil, Bhadrak District, Odisha, situated 26 kilometers west of Bhadrak and near the Brahmani river.
