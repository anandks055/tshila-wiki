## Overview

Hanuman is a powerful and wise monkey warrior, central to the epic Ramayana as well as playing significant roles in the Mahabharata. He is known for his devotion and strength, serving as an ally and advisor to Rama throughout the quest to rescue Sita from the demon king Ravana.

## Role in the Ramayana

Hanuman was created by Pavana (Wind-god) and Maruta, making him a powerful and swift warrior with wisdom. He plays a pivotal role in aiding Rama find and rescue his wife, Sita [1][3]. In Bala Kanda, he helps locate Sita and destroys Lanka after returning with proof of her safety [2].

Hanuman serves as a counselor to Sugriva, showcasing eloquence and noble attributes while investigating the intentions of Rama and Lakshmana [4-7]. He leads missions within Sugriva's army, advising on strategic matters and representing their alliance in rituals [8-13].

In Sundara Kanda, Hanuman leaps across the sea to Lanka, searches for Sita, and reassures her with news of Rama’s intentions. Despite facing various challenges, he remains focused on aiding Rama [14-67]. During Yuddha Kanda, he continues as a key advisor and warrior in the final battle against Ravana, supporting Rama throughout the conflict [68-128].

After Ravana's defeat, Hanuman is celebrated for his courage and skill, receiving honors from Rama. He expresses eternal devotion to Rama and agrees to remain on Earth as long as Rama’s story is told [129-133].

## Role in the Mahabharata

In the Mahabharata, Hanuman symbolizes strength and devotion. He grants Bhima a boon for an upcoming battle after they meet in the Himalayas [1]. His image marks Arjuna's chariot during battles, serving as a protector and source of inspiration [2][3][4].

## Geographical References

Hanuman is also the name of several villages in India. One such village is located in Bhagvanpura Tehsil, Khargone District, Madhya Pradesh, known for its temples like Sri Hanuman Mandir Khargone and Sai Mandir Khargone [120][121]. Another village named Hanuman exists in Mandvi Tehsil, Surat District, Gujarat, located 57 kilometers east of Surat. This area is known for its Gujarati-speaking community and nearby rivers like Nagan Delhi [121].

Additionally, there are two more villages named Hanuman in Uchchhal and Vyara Tehsils of Surat District, each with unique local languages and cultural features.
