## Durvasa: Etymology and Location

### Etymological Background

The name **Durvasa** is derived from Sanskrit roots that convey the notion of difficulty or hardship. According to Bhaṭṭācārya's *Vācaspatyam*, "duṣ" (दुः) means suffering, and "vāsa" (वास) refers to dwelling, thus forming a compound meaning one who endures suffering in his abode or is difficult to inhabit [1]. Similarly, Benfey's Sanskrit-English Dictionary defines *durvasa* as “difficult to dwell” [2], indicating that the term carries connotations of hardship and adversity. Cappeller’s dictionary further elucidates this concept by defining it simply as "bad dwelling" [3].

### Geographical Description

Durvasa is also a village located within Mirzapur tehsil in Azamgarh district, Uttar Pradesh, India. It has a postal code of 276304 and is approximately 33 kilometers west of Azamgarh and 11 kilometers from the town of Mirzapur [4]. The village is situated near the rivers Gomti and Tamsa and is surrounded by blocks such as Phulpur, Mirzapur, Pawai, and Tahbarpur. Nearby villages include Banvirpur, Pachhim Patti, and Khuraso.

### Educational Institutions

Educational facilities in Durvasa include notable institutions like Durvasa Public School and Gayadeen Jaiswal Inter College [4].

### Religious Sites

Religious landmarks within the vicinity of Durvasa include Mauni Maharaj Ashram and Shri Durbasa Temple, which are significant to the local community.
