## Overview

Kumbhakarna is a significant figure in Hindu mythology, known as one of Ravana’s brothers and a powerful demon. He is described across various texts, including the Puranas and the Ramayana, as a formidable warrior with immense strength and an insatiable appetite.

### Birth and Lineage
Kumbhakarna was born to Viśravas and his wife Kaikarī (or Keśinī in some versions) [1][5]. According to mythology, he is one of the demons reborn as a result of Jaya and Vijaya's curse from the sages Sanaka and others [1][5].

### Role and Characteristics
Kumbhakarna is known for his immense size, strength, and perpetual sleep, which was granted by Brahma due to his severe penances [2][3]. He assisted Ravana in ruling Lanka and is often depicted as one of Kubera's cousins who defeated him in battle and took over his kingdom [6].

### Key Events
Initially advising Ravana against opposing Rama, Kumbhakarna eventually joined the conflict. His residence was described by Hanuman during his exploration of Lanka, resembling a mass of clouds [1]. He faced defeat in Trijata’s dream and later woke up due to Dhundhu's appearance [3][4].

During the battle with Rama, Kumbhakarna challenged the monkey army but eventually engaged in combat with Lakshmana. He was ultimately defeated by a celestial weapon launched by Rama [5][7]. His fall prompted Ravana to order Indrajit to continue fighting against Rama and his allies [6].

### Death and Aftermath
Kumbhakarna's death left half of his body submerged in the ocean, with his head blocking Lanka’s gateway. This event is celebrated during Ramlila celebrations where his effigy is often burned as a symbol of evil [10][9].
