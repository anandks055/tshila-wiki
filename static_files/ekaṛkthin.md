## Etymology

The term **ekaṛkthin** (also spelled as *ekarikthin* [1]) is derived from Sanskrit components where "eka" means one and "ṛkthin" signifies an heir. This etymological construction indicates a coheir, specifically referring to heirs who share inheritance through the same father [2][3].

## Definitions

**Ekaṛkthin** is defined as a coheir in both sources consulted. According to Bhaṭṭācārya (1900), it signifies an individual who shares inheritance with others from the same parent, specifically their father [2]. Similarly, Wilson's dictionary (1832) also defines **ekaṛkthin** as a coheir, reinforcing its meaning through shared paternity [3].

## Similar Terms

The term is closely related to *ekarikthin* [1], which carries the same connotation of a coheir sharing inheritance from one father.
