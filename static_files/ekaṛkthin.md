## Ekaṛkthin

**Ekaṛkthin** is a term derived from Sanskrit, denoting a coheir [1][2]. The etymology of this word can be broken down into two parts: "eka," meaning one (father), and "ṛkthin," meaning heir. This compound indicates an individual who shares in the inheritance with others [1][2].
