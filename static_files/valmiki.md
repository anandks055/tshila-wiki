## Valmiki

Valmiki is a term in Sanskrit that primarily refers to an anthill. The etymology and usage of this term are found across several lexicons and dictionaries from various periods. According to Vācaspatyam, the term "val-īki" can be derived from the root "bal," which signifies an ant-hill created by a specific type of ant (kīṭa) [1]. This etymological connection is further elaborated in other sources such as Bhāṭṭācārya's Skt-Eng lexicon, where it is defined as "an ant-hill" [2], and similarly noted by Vaidya L R in his Sanskrit-to-English dictionary, referring to the term as synonymous with "valmīka" [3]. Wilson H's dictionary provides a nearly identical definition of Valmiki as an ant-hill, occasionally using the variant spelling "valbhīki" [4]. Lastly, Yates W’s dictionary also defines Valmiki specifically in reference to an anthill, noting it as a noun of the first declension and masculine form [5].
