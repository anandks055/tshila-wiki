## Symbolic Representation in Texts

In various texts such as Yoga Vasistha, Mount Meru serves as a multifaceted symbol with different significances across its chapters. In Book VI - Nirvana prakarana part 1 (nirvana prakarana), Chapter XIV - Narration of Bhusunda and description of Mount Meru, it is described as the central peak where Bhusunda resides [ref3]. The mountain is adorned with gems and serves as a dwelling place for gods and the site of Vasishtha's quest. This description underscores Mount Meru’s role in providing a divine abode for deities and sages.

In Chapter XV - Vasishtha’s visit to Bhusunda, Mount Meru becomes the setting for the description of the scenery, including the Kalpatree and various mythical creatures [ref3]. The Kalpatree, or wish-fulfilling tree, is a significant element in this depiction, symbolizing abundance and prosperity. Moreover, the presence of mythical creatures adds to the mystical aura surrounding Mount Meru, reinforcing its symbolic importance.

In Book VII - Nirvana Prakarana part 2 (nirvana prakarana), Chapter XLV - Continuation of the story of the deerlike mind, Mount Meru symbolizes the pinnacle of spiritual achievement, representing the highest level of enlightenment or godliness [ref3]. This symbolic representation highlights the mountain’s role in achieving spiritual goals and reaching a state of divine consciousness. Additionally, in Chapter CXXXIV - The story of the carcass continued, Mount Meru is where celestial nymphs (Apsaras) dance and where the ocean of blood sparkles like melted gold [ref3]. This vivid imagery serves to enhance the mystical allure of Mount Meru.

In Chapter CLXIV - Unity of the divinity and the mundane world, Mount Meru is mentioned as a place where the illusion of mirage does not occur, symbolizing a state of clarity and absence of delusion [ref3]. This metaphorical use of Mount Meru reinforces its role in dispelling illusions and providing a clear path to enlightenment. Thus, across these chapters, Mount Meru emerges as a multifaceted symbol that encapsulates various spiritual and mystical elements.
