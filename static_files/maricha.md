## Maricha: Overview and Roles in Hindu Mythology

Maricha, also known as Mārīca or Marici, is a multifaceted figure in Hindu mythology, appearing both as a demon and as a divine entity. His primary role involves his deceitful actions leading to significant events such as Sita's abduction by Ravana.

### Role in the Ramayana

According to various sources including the *Ramayana* of Valmiki and Manmatha Nath Dutt’s summary, Maricha is depicted as a rakshasa who disrupts sacrificial rites. He initially serves under Ravana and later aids in tricking Rama by transforming into a golden deer to lure him away from Sita. This act leads to his eventual death at the hands of Rama (sources 4, 7). In the *Ramayana*, Maricha is also described as the son of Taraka and Sunda, becoming a rakshasa due to Agastya's curse (source 8).

### Other Mythological Roles

In the *Brahmanda Purana* and *Skanda Purana*, Maricha is portrayed with more nuanced roles. He is mentioned as the deceitful son of Sunda and Tāḍakā, and also identified as a Prajāpati involved in Śrāddha rituals (source 2). In the *Skanda Purana*, he is described as one of Ravana's advisors who was driven away by Rama from Siddhāśrama and ultimately killed during his exile (source 6).

### Divine Aspects

Maricha also appears in divine contexts. He is recognized in the *Markandeya Purana* as a significant figure, currently regarded as the lord during the Sāvarṇika Manvantara and identified as the father of the gods who will rule alongside Prajāpati Kāśyapa (source 3). Additionally, Maricha is mentioned as a leader among the gods in various contexts within this purana.

### Literary References

In literary works like the *Agni Purana* and *Skanda Purana*, Maricha's actions are further detailed. He is described as being made stupefied by Rama using the Mānava missile, transforming into a golden deer to lure Rama away from Sita, and subsequently killed (source 5). Additionally, in the *Skanda Purana*, he is noted for his involvement in various battles and conflicts alongside Ravana (source 6).

### Summary

Maricha's multifaceted roles span from being a disruptive demon to a divine entity. His most notable action involves aiding Ravana by transforming into a golden deer, leading to Sita’s abduction. He is remembered as a significant antagonist in the *Ramayana*, with his actions and fate intricately tied to the epic events.
