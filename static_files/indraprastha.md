## Introduction

Indraprastha was a capital city built by the Pandavas on the banks of the Yamuna river. It is renowned for its grandeur and beauty, serving as the center from which Yudhishthira and his brothers ruled their kingdom [1][3]. The city is significant in Hindu mythology, particularly within the epic Mahabharata.

## Historical Significance

King Dhritarashtra granted the Pandavas half of the kingdom upon their return to Hastinapura after marrying Draupadi. They desired a suitable capital and approached Vishvakarma, the divine architect, who constructed Indraprastha on the site formerly occupied by the Khāṇḍava forest [1]. This newly built city became the most beautiful of its time, where the Pandavas conducted significant events such as the Rājasuya sacrifice [2][3].

Indraprastha is also notable for being the location where Krishna and Yudhishthira stayed during the completion of the Rājasūya sacrifice [1]. It was here that Arjuna resettled survivors following the Yadava massacre [7]. The city’s strategic importance extended to its northeastern position relative to Vairatanagara, the capital of Matsya country [3].

## Key Events and Residents

Krishna visited Indraprastha frequently and played a pivotal role in various events such as the Rājasūya sacrifice [6][8]. Additionally, significant incidents like Arjuna’s abduction of Subhadra occurred here [25], making it a central location for many critical episodes in the Mahabharata.

The city's opulence was not only celebrated but also caused jealousy among enemies such as Duryodhana. The gambling match that led to the loss of the kingdom took place at Indraprastha, and after losing, the Pandavas eventually left it behind [9][11]. Bhishma suggested using Indraprastha as a means to peace between the Pandavas and Kauravas [12].

## Modern Context

Today, the old city of Delhi is located at the site where Indraprastha once stood. It is now part of Central Delhi, with nearby localities such as Mathura Road and Bahadur Shah Zafar Marg. The area retains educational institutions like Jain Sr Sec School and Happy School [9]. There are also several temples within short distances from Indraprastha.

## Conclusion

Indraprastha remains a significant location in Hindu mythology and Indian history, symbolizing the grandeur of ancient kingdoms and serving as a focal point for numerous pivotal events described in the Mahabharata. The city’s modern legacy can still be observed through various landmarks and institutions within Central Delhi.
