## ārabdhaṃ

Ārabdhaṃ (आरब्धं) is a Sanskrit term derived from the root आङ् + रभ् + क्तः [1][2]. The term has multiple meanings within Indic knowledge traditions.

### Meanings and Usage

1. **कृतारम्भं (Kṛtārambhaṃ)**: This signifies an action or ritual that has been initiated but not completed.
   - Example from Malamasa Tattva [1]: 
     “आरब्धं मलमासात् प्राक् यत् कर्म्म न समापितं ।
      आगते मलमासेऽपि तत् समाप्यं न संशयः” 
     This verse indicates that if an action is initiated before the month of Malamāsa and remains incomplete, it should be completed upon reaching Malamāsa [1].

2. **Ārabdhakarmṇyaśaucābhāvo**: This refers to a lack of ritual impurity (शौच) in actions that have been started.
   - Example from Tithyaditattva [1]:
     “व्रतयज्ञविवाहेषु श्राद्धे होमेऽर्च्चने जपे ।
      आरब्धे सूतकं न स्यादनारब्धे तु सूतकं”
     This verse suggests that in actions such as vows, sacrifices, weddings, ancestral offerings, oblations, worship, and chanting, once they are initiated (आरब्धे), there should be no ritual impurity; whereas if not started (अनारब्धे), the situation remains ritually impure [1].
