## Vibhīṣaṇa

Vibhīṣaṇa is a significant figure in Indic knowledge traditions and is known for his devotion to Rāma. He is the youngest brother of Rāvaṇa, Śurpaṇakhā, and Kumbhakarṇa [1]. In the Rāmarahasya Upaniṣad, Vibhīṣaṇa questioned the method to destroy heinous sins and received guidance on repeating Rāma's name [2]. He is also noted for singing hymns in praise of Rāma in the Adhyātma Rāmāyana [3].

Vibhīṣaṇa deserted Rāvaṇa to join Rāma, providing invaluable assistance and ensuring Rāma’s victory and the restoration of dharma [4]. After killing Rāvaṇa, he offered the Puṣpaka to Rāma to help him return to Ayodhyā [5]. Vibhīṣaṇa is associated with a temple site in Dhanuṣkoti and is said to have visited many pilgrimage sites according to sthalapurāṇas [6][7].

He is also recognized for carrying the icon of Rañganātha from Ayodhyā to Laṅkā but leaving it at Śrīraṅgam [8][9]. Vibhīṣaṇa helped Hanumān locate Sītā after recognizing her presence in Laṅkā [10].

In the Garuda Purana, he is installed as the ruler of Lanka by Rama after Ravana's defeat [15] and is noted for defecting to Rama’s side, aligning himself with the forces of righteousness [16]. According to the Mahabharata, Vibhīṣaṇa is a virtuous and pious Rakshasa born to Malini who follows the righteous path and is granted immortality by Brahma [18].

Vibhīṣaṇa advises Rama and is installed as the new king of Lanka after Ravana’s defeat [19]. He assists Rama's army by guiding them across the ocean and serving as Rama’s counselor [20], while also helping Lakshmana breach the southern gate of Lanka [21]. During battles, he uses celestial weapons to reveal the invisibility of Ravana’s forces and fights valiantly against formidable opponents like Prahasta and Dhumraksha [22][23].

Throughout the conflict, Vibhīṣaṇa plays a crucial role in awakening Rama and Lakshmana, bringing healing water from Kuvera, and guiding Lakshmana in fighting against Indrajit [24]. Additionally, he encourages Rama to use Indra’s chariot to defeat Ravana [25] and continues to support Rama even after Ravana’s defeat [26]. In another context, Vibhīṣaṇa is mentioned as one of the Yakshas who waits upon Kuvera in his mansion [27].

In the Brahma Purana, Vibhīṣaṇa is noted along with Indra and Rāma as having worshipped Viṣṇu [29]. He is celebrated as the foremost amongst demons, paralleling Puruṣottama’s sanctity and preeminence [30]. Furthermore, he is identified as Rāvana's sibling and an ardent follower of Nārāyaṇa; he venerates the statue of Vāsudeva in Laṅkā, earning numerous favors and benedictions through this worship [31].

According to the Ramayana, Vibishana initially aids Rama and is later enthroned as the king of Lanka [33]. He defects to Rama’s side, playing a crucial role in aiding Rama in the defeat of Ravana [34].

In the Bhagavata Purana, he is one of the sons of Viśravas and his wife Keśinī [36] and is known as a loyal brother of Rāvaṇa who defects to Rāma’s side, aiding in the defeat of Rāvaṇa [37].

In the Agni Purana, Vibhīṣaṇa is portrayed as a character deeply rooted in dharma and a loyal demon who assists Rāma. He advises against harming Hanūmat and later becomes an ally of Rāma, being appointed ruler of Laṅkā [39]. He consoles the demons after Rāvaṇa’s death and cremates him [40], emphasizing his adherence to dharma [41]. Additionally, he is invoked to destroy evil demons in Chapter 31 - Mode of cleansing oneself and others [42].
