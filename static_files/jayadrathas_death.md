Jayadratha's Death refers to the event in the Mahabharata where he is killed by Arjuna on the 13th day of the Kurukshetra war. According to the epic, Jayadratha was responsible for holding back the Pandavas while Drona attacked Abhimanyu. As a result, Arjuna vows to kill Jayadratha before sunset or commit suicide. On the next day, Arjuna beheads Jayadratha with a divine weapon, fulfilling his vow.
