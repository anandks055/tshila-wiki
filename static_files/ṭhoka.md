## Etymology

The term **ṭhōka** (ಠೋಕ) is a Kannada word that has multiple meanings and usages.

## Shruti

In the context of language, **ṭhōka** can be understood as an adjective or a noun in Kannada. As an adjective, it translates to "ठೋಕು1" [1], while as a noun, it is rendered as "ಠೋಕು2" [1].

## Smriti

The term **ṭhōka** also has geographical significance, referring to several villages across India. In Maharashtra, **Toka** (a variant spelling of ṭhōka) is a village located in the Nevasa tehsil of Ahmednagar district. It is situated approximately 74 km north of Ahmednagar and is notable for its proximity to temples such as Sidheshwar Temple and Shiv Mandir, as well as rivers like Yirabhadra Pravara [2].

## Purana

In Haryana, **Toka** exists in two locations. One village is situated in Ambala district near Shri Guru Ravidas Ji Temple and the Dabar Ghagar river. The other is located in Panchkula district, close to the Neri Khad Shyok River and neighboring cities like Panchkula and Chandigarh [2].

## Itihasa

In Uttar Pradesh, **Toka** is found in Bahraich district, surrounded by blocks such as Phakharpur. This village is also near rivers like the Ghaghara [2].
