## Chanura: An Asura Wrestler

**Chanura**, also known as **Cāṇūra**, is an Asura character prominently featured in various Hindu Puranas. He serves as a wrestler and a friend of Kamsa, aiding him in his efforts to eliminate the threat posed by Krishna and Balarāma (Ref: 1).

According to the Puranas, Chanura was one of several wrestlers sent by Kamsa to challenge Krishna and Balarāma to a match. In the Harivamsha Purana, he is described as an enemy of the gods destined for defeat by Madhusudana (Krishna) during a wrestling contest arranged in the arena (Ref: 3). Similarly, the Vishnu Purana and Bhagavata Purana recount that Chanura was specifically chosen to fight against Krishna but was eventually defeated and killed by him (Refs: 4, 6).

In addition to his role as an opponent in the wrestling matches, Chanura is also mentioned in other contexts. He is listed among the demons slain during various battles fought by Krishna, such as those involving Naraka (Ref: 4). The Brahma Purana highlights that Chanura's defeat symbolizes divine power and moral righteousness, with mantras dedicated to Krishna commemorating his victory over wickedness (Ref: 5).

Chanura is consistently portrayed as a formidable wrestler appointed by Kamsa to eliminate threats against the Yadus. However, he ultimately meets his end at the hands of Krishna during their fateful wrestling match in the arena.
