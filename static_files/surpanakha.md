## Surpanakha

Surpanakha is known in Hindu mythology as the sister of Ravana. The name "Surpanakha" is derived from Sanskrit, where *sūrpa* means winnowing basket and *nakha* means nail. This etymology suggests her nails were shaped like a winnowing basket, which may allude to her fierce or monstrous appearance.

In the epic Ramayana, Surpanakha plays a significant role as Ravana's sister, though specific details about her actions beyond this familial relation are limited within the provided sources.
